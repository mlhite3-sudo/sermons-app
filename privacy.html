Privacy Policy

Sermons for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 7 November 2026

Sermons does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally so it can remember where you were. This never leaves your iPhone or iPad, is never sent anywhere, and is not visible to the developer:

  • Which sermon you were listening to and your position in it
  • Which sermons you have finished
  • Sermons you have saved
  • Your appearance and playback-speed preferences

All of it is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising identifier, no App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • No crash-reporting or telemetry services
  • No third-party software development kits
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location
  • No data shared with or sold to anyone

Audio streaming

Sermon text and the app itself are stored inside the app and work without a network connection. Audio is the exception: it streams from cdn.gty.org, which is operated by Grace to You, not by this app. As with loading any web address, their servers can see your device's IP address and which audio file was requested. That connection is made directly by your device and nothing about it is reported back to this app or its developer. Grace to You's own privacy practices govern what they do with their server logs.

Links out of the app

A few buttons open gty.org in Safari. Once Safari takes over, Apple's and Grace to You's privacy terms apply, not this one.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.
