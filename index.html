Common questions

Does it work without a signal?
Yes, almost entirely. Every sermon title, Scripture reference, series and transcript is stored inside the app. Only the audio itself streams, so you can browse, search and read anywhere — you just need a connection to play.

Does it keep playing when the screen locks?
Yes, with full lock-screen and CarPlay controls.

Why do some sermons not have a transcript?
1,635 of them do. The rest are still being added.

I lost my place.
Progress is stored on the device and survives updates, but it is deleted if you delete the app, and it does not yet sync between devices.

How do I start somewhere other than the beginning?
On the Home screen, open Years to cover and set the range, then tap Start listening. You can also use Jump to # to go to a specific position in the run.
